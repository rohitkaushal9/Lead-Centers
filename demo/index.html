Common questions

Do I need to pay anything to see the demo?

No. The demo is free and there is no card required. You can also start the 3-day free trial without speaking to anyone if you would rather explore on your own.

Will you show me real leads or a canned recording?

Real leads, live. We open the current queue and filter it to your categories, budget bands and regions so you can judge the quality yourself. Contact details stay masked until you are on a plan.

How long does the call take?

Twenty minutes is usually enough. If your team wants a deeper session on CRM sync, webhooks or the API, we will book a longer follow-up.

What if the leads are not right for my agency?

Then we will tell you. If your services or target budgets fall outside what our queue reliably carries, it is better for both sides to say so on the call than to sell you a plan you will cancel.

Would rather just try it yourself?

Skip the call. The 3-day free trial needs no card and takes under a minute to set up.
